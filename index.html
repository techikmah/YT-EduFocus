One-Click Focus Mode

Toggle from the popup or the inline button beside Like/Share. Hides comments, dims distracting UI, adds a focus glow around the player. Works instantly, no reload needed.

🍅

Pomodoro Sessions

Set a 25, 45, or 60-minute study session. Get a system notification when it ends so you know when to take a break without watching the clock.

⏱

Study Timer & Streak

Tracks your learning time per session and per day. A 7-day chart in the popup shows your weekly progress. Streaks keep you coming back.

📝

Per-Video Notes

A slide-in notes panel auto-saves as you type, stored per video. Export everything as a .txt file whenever you need it.

📖

Chapter Navigator

Extracts timestamps from the video description and lists them in the sidebar so you can jump to any chapter in one click.

⚡

Speed Controls

0.75× to 2× playback speed, accessible directly in the sidebar or with the Alt+S keyboard shortcut to cycle through.

🚫

Homepage & Shorts Blocker

Optionally block the YouTube homepage and Shorts entirely while Focus Mode is active, replacing them with a clean redirect to educational search.

How It Works

Three steps.
Zero friction.

1

Install the Extension

Add YT EduFocus from the Chrome Web Store in one click. No account, no sign-up, no email. It works immediately.

2

Open Any YouTube Video

Navigate to any YouTube video. The extension detects if it's educational and shows the 🎓 icon in your Chrome toolbar.

3

Activate Focus Mode

Click the toggle or press Alt+F. Distractions disappear and the sidebar fills with educational content matched to your topic.
